Our Story

A Place to Slow Down

What started as a tiny cart on Belmont Street in 2018 has grown into something we never quite imagined. KOPPEE was born from a simple belief: that a great cup of coffee can change the rhythm of your entire day.

We partner directly with farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala to source the finest single-origin beans. Every batch is roasted in-house, right here in Portland, to bring out the distinct flavors each region has to offer.
