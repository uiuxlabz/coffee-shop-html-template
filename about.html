Our Story

How a small cart on Belmont Street became Portland's favorite corner for great coffee and honest conversation.

From Cart to Corner

In the spring of 2018, two friends with a shared obsession for single-origin coffee packed up a secondhand espresso machine, a hand grinder, and more optimism than sense, and set up a cart on Belmont Street.

What began as weekend pop-ups quickly turned into something bigger. Word spread about the Ethiopian Yirgacheffe pour-over, the homemade syrups, and the fact that we remembered every customer's name after their second visit.

By 2020, we had outgrown the cart. We found a permanent home just three blocks away — a sun-filled space with reclaimed wood, exposed brick, and room for the roaster we'd been dreaming about. We roast every batch in-house now, working directly with farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala to bring the best beans to Portland.

KOPPEE has never been about being the biggest. It's about being the place you actually want to sit down, slow down, and enjoy a cup that someone cared about making.

What We Believe

Our Values

These aren't just words on a wall — they guide every decision we make, from bean selection to the last dish we plate.

🌱

Sustainability First

We source directly from farmers who practice sustainable agriculture, compost our grounds, and use compostable packaging throughout the shop.

🤝

Community Matters

We partner with local bakeries, host neighborhood events, and keep our space open for anyone who needs a warm corner and a good cup.

Craft Over Convenience

We never rush the pour. Every drink is made with intention, from hand-pulled shots to slow-steamed milk. Quality takes the time it takes.
